# Cover & Title Hook

## Principle
Push the real conflict or stakes in the content to their sharpest honest form. The hook is the extreme of what the facts actually support — the body then explains the nuance. Never upgrade an allegation, rumour or possibility into a stated fact in a title.

## Workflow
1. Extract from the content: the strongest contrast, the biggest concrete number, the most surprising subject, and the open question readers will want answered.
2. Produce 6–8 titles in mixed forms: statement, contrast, number-led, and at most two questions. Never all questions.
3. For each title, note in brackets which fact supports it. Drop any title you cannot support.
4. Cover text: compress the chosen title to a cover version — ≤ 11 characters per line, ≤ 3 lines, keep one number or key subject highlighted. It must stay readable at thumbnail size and inside the central 1:1 crop.
5. Ban list: no agency-brief prefixes like "国家：……" / "快讯："; no "震惊/紧急" empty shock words; no war/outcome predictions stated as fact.

## Output
Title options grouped by form, the recommended one first with a one-line reason, plus the cover-line version of the recommended title.
