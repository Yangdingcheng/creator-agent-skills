# Xiaohongshu Note

## Inputs
- The source content or topic, plus the target reader (who is it for, what problem it solves)

## Workflow
1. Titles: give 5 options, each ≤ 20 characters where possible, with a concrete number, result or contrast. No empty hype words repeated across all options.
2. Body: hook in the first 2 lines (a result, a mistake, or a contrast). Short lines, one point per line group, personal and specific — name the tool, step or number instead of "亲测有效" generalities.
3. Card plan: 4–8 cards at 3:4 (1080×1440). Card 1 = cover: big title (≤ 14 characters per line, max 3 lines) + one highlight. Cards 2..n-1 = one point each (title + ≤ 4 short lines). Last card = summary + a question to invite comments.
4. Hashtags: 5–8, mixing one broad tag, topic tags and one long-tail tag. No unrelated trending tags.

## Hard rules
- Do not fabricate personal experience the user did not claim ("我用了一年" when they did not).
- Medical, financial and efficacy claims need a source or must be softened to personal observation.
- Keep every claim consistent with the source content; the note is an adaptation, not a rewrite into different facts.

## Output
Titles list, body text ready to paste, card-by-card text plan (ready to feed to a card generator), hashtags line.
