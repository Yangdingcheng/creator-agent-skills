# Daily News Brief

## Workflow
1. Scan the day's major international events across wires and primary outlets. Prefer primary/domestic primary-source reporting for confirmation; treat single-source foreign reports as leads, not facts.
2. Deduplicate: the same event from five outlets is one topic. Merge updates into the existing topic instead of creating a new one.
3. Rank by: how many readers it affects, how much genuinely changed today, and whether there is a clear "why it matters". Drop pure noise (statements with no action, recycled anniversaries).
4. For each surviving topic output: a working title, 3–6 verified fact points (each with its source), what is still unconfirmed, and the angle (what changed / who is affected / what to watch).
5. Election/vote topics: label results as preliminary until the official body confirms the final count; a candidate refusing to concede is reported as such, never as a concession.

## Hard rules
- Fresh events only: today's developments, not explainers of old topics dressed as news.
- Never present an allegation as fact; attribute claims to who made them.
- If a key fact cannot be confirmed from an openable source, keep the topic but flag the gap explicitly — do not fill it in.

## Output
A ranked topic list (7–8 for a daily batch), each topic ready to hand to a writing skill: title, fact points with sources, unconfirmed items, angle.
