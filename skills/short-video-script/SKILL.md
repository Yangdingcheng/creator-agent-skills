# Short Video Script

## Structure (60 seconds ≈ 220–260 Chinese characters of narration)
1. 0–3s Hook: one sentence with the strongest fact, number or contrast. No greeting, no self-introduction first.
2. 3–15s Setup: what happened / what the problem is, in plain words.
3. 15–50s Body: 2–3 points, each introduced by its on-screen keyword. One point per breath.
4. 50–60s Turn + close: what is still unknown or what to watch next, then one specific question for comments (not "你怎么看" alone — give the two sides).

## Deliverables
- Narration script with time marks per segment
- On-screen text per segment (≤ 12 characters each — subtitles are not the narration)
- Cover text: ≤ 3 lines, one highlight number/subject, fits 9:16 safe area (keep key text in the middle band)
- B-roll / footage suggestions per segment (real scenes over stock clichés)

## Hard rules
- Facts follow the source: no invented numbers or quotes; disputed claims attributed in narration ("据报道").
- No outcome predictions as fact. End on the open question, not a fake conclusion.
- Read the script aloud mentally: any sentence you cannot finish in one breath gets split.
